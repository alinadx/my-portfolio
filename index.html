You

How do you usually work?

Alina

First, I learn about your project, goals, audience, timeline, and requirements.

Then I research, define the direction, and create the first concepts or prototype.

We review the work together, refine the design based on your feedback, and prepare the final solution.

I keep the process clear with regular updates, so you always know what stage we’re at.
